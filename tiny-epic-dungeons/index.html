Tiny Epic Dungeons Rules

A cooperative dungeon crawl. Explore the dungeon, kill every Minion, find the Lair Door and defeat the Boss before the torch burns out.

1–4 players (2–4 heroes)~45–60 minCooperative

1How you win or lose

You win

Defeat the Boss in Act 2, inside its Lair.

You lose if any of these happen
  • A 5th Goblin has to be placed.
  • The torch reaches the skull at the end of its track.
  • No open corridors are left to explore and the Lair Door hasn't been found.

Hero attributes
  • Health: below 1 means you're unconscious.
  • Focus: pays for abilities and die changes. It can't go below 0.
  • Speed: the most rooms you can enter in a turn.
  • Defense: how much damage you cancel from a counter-attack.
  • Skills: Strength, Agility, Intellect: how many dice you roll for a check (always 1–3, never a 4th).
Skill checks
  • Roll as many Hero dice as your skill (1–3).
  • Pick one die as your result. +1/+2 modifiers add to it.
  • If you succeed, unused dice showing Focus give you Focus, and unused 6s give you Health.
  • Attacks also roll the Enemy die when the target can see you (same room or line of sight).
Check for danger (newly revealed rooms)
  • Goblin icon: take 1 damage and stop. Draw a Goblin onto the Torch mat and put its token in that room.
  • Minion icon: take 2 damage and stop. Draw a Minion, set its Health marker to the top of its track, and put its token in the room.
  • Trap: make the evasion check on the left side of the card, and take the damage shown if you fail. Then you can keep moving or try to disarm it.
Attacking and damage
  • Melee: a target in your room. Missile: a target in another room in line of sight. Spell: targets within the spell's range. Spell damage ignores Defense.
  • Damage dealt = your check total minus the enemy's Defense.
  • Goblins have 1 Health. Minions track Health on their card.
  • Killing a Minion: gain loot or spells twice, move the torch up 6 (never above its start), and put its Health marker on the Boss mat.
Enemy counter-attack

If the enemy survives and can see you, it hits back using the Enemy die:

  • 2–5: the die plus the enemy's modifier for your attack type.
  • Shield: just the enemy's modifier as damage.
  • Torch: a miss, but the torch moves down 1.

Subtract your Defense. Whatever is left is the damage you take.

Unconscious heroes
  • At 0 Health your turn ends. Lay your miniature down.
  • On your next turn you must Rest, then you may move. No Free Actions that turn.
  • Healing from another hero wakes you up right away.
  • Starting your turn unconscious alone with a monster does not lose the game. That was a pre-release rule.
Loot, spells and equipment limits
  • You can only equip a card when an effect lets you discover it or pick it up. You can discard one at any time as a Free Action.
  • Limits: 1 Garb, 2 Hand, 2 Spells, 2 Trinkets.
  • Pieces of a Legendary Set give a bonus from 2 pieces, and more as you collect more.
Enemy movement (Goblins and Minions)
  • Goblins attack if a hero is in their room. Otherwise they move. Each one deals damage equal to the number of Goblins in play, plus 1 if it's attacking.
  • Minions move toward the nearest conscious hero by the shortest path, stop at the first room with a hero, then attack.
Entering the Lair (Act 2)

When the Lair Door is revealed and every Minion is dead, step from the Lair Door onto the Boss mat's entrance (1 Speed). Then:

  1. Flip the Boss mat to Act 2. Stack all Minion Health markers on the 8 space.
  2. Place the Boss token in the Lair.
  3. Flip the Torch mat to Act 2 and put the torch on the top space for your hero count.
  4. Place Altar markers on each Minion encounter.
The Boss fight
  • Heroes stand on Hero Spaces to attack. Some spaces give a bonus, but only when you end your movement there. Only 1 hero per space.
  • Each Minion Health marker takes damage separately. A marker can't pass an uncovered Altar.
  • When the Boss enters an Altar room, it moves to the highest uncovered Altar and that marker comes off.
  • Taunt (2 Focus, repeatable) pulls the Boss toward you.
  • Missile and spell attacks from outside can't reach the Boss in its Lair. You can leave the Lair through its entrance.
  • In Act 2, when the torch passes an Enemy Action, the Boss acts after the Goblins.
Solo play

Control at least 2 heroes, tracking each one's stats, gear and position separately. Your hero count, not your player count, sets the setup numbers.

FAQ quick hits
  • Do I have to do anything on my turn? No, but the torch still moves down.
  • Can I act in any order? Yes. Move, Heroic Action and Free Actions can happen in any order.
  • Free Actions on someone else's turn? No.
  • Same Free Action twice? Yes, if you can pay for it.
  • Torch higher than its start? Never.
