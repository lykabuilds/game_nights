Century: Golem Edition Rules

Collect and upgrade magic crystals, add Merchant cards to your hand, and spend crystals on golems worth points. A light deck-builder: your hand is your engine.

2Crystal ladder

Yellow→Green→Blue→Pink

Each upgrade moves one crystal up one step. With “Upgrade 2” you can turn two yellows into two greens, or one yellow into a blue. At game end, every non-yellow crystal left in your caravan is worth 1 point.

3Your turn: pick exactly ONE action

4Rules detail click to expand

Caravan limit (10 crystals)

You can go over 10 during your turn. At the end of your turn, discard down to 10, keeping whichever crystals you like.

Crystal supply

The crystal supply is unlimited. If a color runs out, use something else as a stand-in.

Trade cards – repeating trades

One play of a Trade card lets you make the exchange as many times as you can pay for it. Example: a “2 yellow → 1 blue” card with 6 yellows can give you 3 blues.

Acquiring – paying the cards to the left

To take the 4th card, put one crystal each on cards 1, 2 and 3. Crystals left on a card go to whoever takes it later, so cheap cards can come with free crystals.

Coins

Gold = 3 points, silver = 1 point. You only get one when you claim the golem in the 1st (gold) or 2nd (silver) spot. Once the gold is gone, silver moves to the 1st spot and the 2nd spot pays nothing.

Strategy tips
  • Early on, cheap Upgrade and good Trade cards build a strong engine.
  • Don't rest too often. Each rest is a turn without crystals.
  • Keep an eye on the coins. Grabbing the 1st golem is worth 3 extra points.
  • Leftover blue and pink crystals score at the end, so don't waste them.

5Game end and winning

The end is triggered when a player claims their…

  • 5th golem with 4–5 players
  • 6th golem with 2–3 players

Finish the current round so everyone has the same number of turns.

Scoring

Golem points + coins (gold 3, silver 1) + 1 point per non-yellow crystal.

Tie: the tied player who went later in turn order wins.
