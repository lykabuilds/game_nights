Harmonies Rules

Build a landscape out of colored tokens, then create the right habitats so animals can settle in it. Points come from your terrain and from the animals you settle.

Mountains (gray stacks)

A stack of 1–3 gray tokens. It only scores if it's next to at least one other mountain. A lone mountain scores 0.

Fields (yellow groups)

Each group of 2 or more connected yellow tokens scores 5 points, no matter how big it is. Several small groups beat one big group.

Buildings (red on top)

A red token on top of a gray, brown or red token is a building. It scores 5 points if the spaces around it show at least 3 different colors (look at the top token of each stack).

Water – Side A: Rivers

Score only your single longest river: a chain of connected blue tokens, counted end to end.

Water – Side B: Islands

Blue tokens divide your board into separate land areas. Each island (a group of non-water tokens cut off from the others by water or the board edge) scores 5 points.

Animal cards and habitats
  • Each card shows a habitat pattern (token colors and heights) and a track of cubes with point values.
  • The pattern must match exactly, including stack heights. You can rotate it.
  • The cube goes on the highlighted token of the pattern.
  • At game end, each card scores the highest point value you've uncovered on its track. Unfinished cards still score what you've uncovered.
  • Cube placement is final, even if later tokens change the area around it.
Card limit

You can have at most 4 unfinished cards in front of you. Once every cube on a card is placed, set the card aside. It no longer counts toward the limit.

Nature's Spirit variant

Recommended once you know the game. At setup each player draws Spirit cards and keeps one. A Spirit card works like an Animal card with a single cube. Once its cube is placed, it gives a special end-game scoring bonus. It counts toward the 4-card limit until it's finished.

Solo mode

Harmonies includes a solo mode where you play to beat score targets. The rulebook covers the small setup and refill changes. Keep your rulebook nearby for the exact solo steps.

5Game end and winning

The end is triggered when…

  • the pouch can't refill the central board, or
  • at the end of a player's turn, their board has 2 or fewer empty spaces.

Finish the round so everyone has played the same number of turns.

Winner

Most points wins.

Tie: the tied player who placed the most Animal cubes wins. If still tied, they share the victory.
